# Charlestown House of Pizza: redesign concept

Built Oct 29, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated food images.

## Files

- `preview.html`: the whole site in one file, with a "design concept" ribbon. Open it in any browser to show the owner.
- `charlestown-house-of-pizza-netlify-site.zip`: the launch-ready site (no ribbon). Unzip and drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## Design concept (v2)

Drawn parallax hero (Main Street storefront with a soft-serve cone sign, sun, clouds, hills) plus the **Soft-Serve Sundae Maker**: build a cone, waffle cone, dish, sundae, flurry, float or milkshake from the shop's real 26-flavor list and see it drawn. Cream, cherry red and pine green; Young Serif + Smooch Sans + Lora.

## What we found on the current site

- Specials & Coupons page is stock template filler (“Delicious Turkish Pide Pizza, up to 33.50% off”), unrelated to the shop.
- The Rewards page is an empty “Coming Soon”.
- The job-application link points to the Google Form editor URL (/edit), not the public form.
- The home page is a grid of identical “Order Online” cards.
- The shop’s best-kept asset, a 26-flavor soft-serve counter, is a single line of text.

## What the new site does about it

- No real food photos were available, so every illustration is drawn, none AI-generated.
- Live open/closed from the posted hours (7 days, 10:30 AM to 9 PM), paged menu with search, catering inquiry form (Netlify Forms), DoorDash and online-order buttons, job link, mobile order bar.
- The fake template specials and the empty Rewards page are left out instead of replaced with invented offers.
- Search-engine basics: page title and description, Restaurant structured data, sitemap and robots.txt.

## Questions for the owner before launch

1. Do you run real specials or coupons? The concept leaves the specials area out rather than invent offers.
2. Are Rewards still planned? The concept leaves them out.
3. Should the job link be the public Google Form (we used the same form ID with /viewform; please confirm)?
4. Where should catering inquiries go (email address)? The concept form is set up for Netlify Forms.
5. Ice cream prices are not on your site. Want them shown?
6. Spelling fixes we made: “Brocoli”, “Oinion”, “Cheeseburge”, “Jalepeno”.
7. Do flurries come on vanilla soft serve? We assumed yes for the sundae maker.
