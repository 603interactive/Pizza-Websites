From the kitchen

Our story

A Brooklyn girl, a Belmont pizza shop, and the boys.

From the shop's own history, lightly tidied.

  1. 1984

    Christine, born and raised in Brooklyn, relocates to New Hampshire. Living in the Loudon and Canterbury area, she and her husband Frank meet Billy and Sandy, who have just opened the original Brookside Pizza.

  2. 1999

    Christine joins forces with the Lazos family and starts learning the business. A year later she moves to the Belmont location.

  3. 2003

    Offered a partnership, Christine jumps at it.

  4. 2006

    In April, Brookside Pizza II opens The Tap Public House.

  5. 2009

    In January a break in the sprinkler water main forces the business to close. It reopens in May, with thanks to customers, friends and crew.

  6. Today

    Most days you'll find her sons Michael and David and the rest of the crew hustling out the good food Brookside is known for.

In the community

Brookside backs the Belmont DARE program, the Belknap County Sportsmen's Club and TEAM FEED, and donated ballistic helmets to the Belmont Police Department.
