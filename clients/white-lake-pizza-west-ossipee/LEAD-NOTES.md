# White Lake Pizza (West Ossipee, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 05, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos added by us.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (whitelakepizza.com, Squarespace)
- Untouched 2018 Squarespace template; homepage is a logo, two one-line banners and a wall of hours and payment rules, with no photos of the shop or food.
- Menu is split across seven separate nav pages with no single menu view, and each price is printed twice around the item name.
- "Careers" page (linked from "now hiring!") is empty apart from the tax disclaimer.
- Summer and off-season hours are both shown, so visitors have to work out which applies.
- Online ordering (VersiEats) is only a banner, not a clear button.
- No about section, no social links, "Powered by Squarespace" footer.

## The idea: one menu, two seasons of hours
The shop has two sets of hours (summer, and spring/fall/winter) plus a separate dining-room closing time and daily slice hours. The new site puts the whole menu on one page with tabs and search (all seven sections, 131 items, prices from the shop's menu), shows a live Open/Closed status that also says when the dining room closes, and lets visitors flip the hours table between Summer and Spring/Fall/Winter. The sign's own colors (red, blue) and slab lettering drive the look; the cartoon chef logo is the brand mark.
