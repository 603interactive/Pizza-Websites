    Family-owned

    Sam, Sharon and the whole Galatas family

    Golden Acres Pizza Market has been a proud member of the Goffstown community serving hearty pizza since 1998. The restaurant is owned and operated by Serafim (Sam) and Sharon Galatas. Sam came to the U.S. to pursue the American Dream from a small village about four hours from Athens.

    Over the years almost every member of the Galatas family has helped make Golden Acres a success. Some of our customers have been coming in regularly since the day we opened, and we sponsor many local sports teams. We hope to feed you for years to come.
