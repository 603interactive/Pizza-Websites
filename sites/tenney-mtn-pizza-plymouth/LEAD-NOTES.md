# Tenney Mountain Pizza (Plymouth, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 04, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos. Prices are NOT invented: the shop publishes none.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (drag onto app.netlify.com/drop).

## What we found on the current site (tenneymtnpizza.com)
- GoDaddy Website Builder site, copyright 2021; shop name in lowercase ("tenney mtn pizza") and cut off in the footer.
- Homepage is one sentence repeated five times; About Us page is empty; Photo Gallery and Social sections nearly empty.
- Menu pages show almost no prices. Builder placeholders left live ("Add a footnote...", "Add a description about this item" twice).
- Phone-only ordering; "delivery subject to driver availability". Typos: "Ceaser", "Chedder", "French" listed twice.
- A spammy "Unofficial Website" (tenneymountainpizza.com) with fake text and euro prices competes in search; it is not the shop's.

## The one big idea: the Trail Order Sheet
No online ordering and a 110-item menu with no prices. The site turns the menu into an order sheet: build a pizza (size, red/white sauce, cheese/two/three topping from the shop's own topping list) or tap + Add on any item, search all items, then tap-to-call or copy the sheet to a text. Stored only in the visitor's browser. It totals only the white-sauce add-ons the shop actually printed ($1.50/$2.25 pizza, $2.50/$3.25 special pizza, $0.95/$1.75 calzone); every base price says "call for pricing". "Additional toppings" shows the shop's $4.00 as listed.

## Hours (honest handling)
Only "Open today 11:00 am - 08:00 pm" is known. The site shows that as today's listing and says weekly hours are not published, call to confirm. No live open/closed badge, no hours in structured data.
