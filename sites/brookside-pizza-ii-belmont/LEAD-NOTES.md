# Brookside Pizza II & The Tap Public House (Belmont, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 04, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site
- Squarespace site (~2016-17) under the bar's corporate name; the pizza shop has no identity of its own.
- "Brookside Pizza II Menu" page has no menu, only the address; the Tap menu page shows leftover block labels.
- The only real menu is two crooked phone photos of the paper menu, plus 2017 catering scans with "to Upload" in file names.
- Sidebar on every page says the business is open 11AM-12AM daily; the paper menu says the restaurant closes at 9 or 10 PM and only the bar runs to midnight.
- No online ordering; three phone numbers and no clear call button. About text has typos and reads like 2009. Merch/bar photos dominate.

## The one big idea
"What's open right now": a live Day Dial showing, hour by hour for any day of the week, when slices (until 3 PM), the kitchen (Mon-Thu/Sun 11-9, Fri-Sat 11-10) and the Tap bar (until midnight) are going, with a live "now" line and countdowns. It fixes the wrong-hours problem and explains the two-businesses-one-building setup.

## Also on the site
Full transcribed menu with prices (12 sections, search + tabs), tap-to-call primary CTA (no online ordering exists), three phone numbers, directions link (maps search; no verified coordinates so no embed), family story timeline, community section, Tap photos, catering contact, restaurant/bar hours table, sticky mobile call bar, Restaurant structured data.
