    Teddy's Lounge

    The bar is part of the building. Order out, or stop by the lounge for dinner. The shop's own Facebook page says the lounge opens daily at 10:30 AM, and posts burger nights, football on the TVs and a Coors Light chalkboard.

    Parking: the lot behind the shop, or along the railroad tracks by the train station. For pickup, take Epping Street to the side road.

    Lounge closing time isn't posted. Call 603-895-9500.

    Catering available

    Feed the office, the party, the team.

    The shop caters holiday office and home parties. For larger orders, call in by 10:30 AM so they can plan the travel time. Business delivery runs all day. Catering prices are by call.

    Salads

    Tossed, Chef (ham, salami, cheese, turkey), Chicken Deluxe (marinated grilled chicken, mozzarella) and Greek. Small tray serves 6 to 10, large 12 to 15.

    Sandwich platters

    Classic (roast beef, ham, smoked turkey, American, Swiss), Italian (ham, Genoa salami, provolone), Wrap platter (chicken salad, tuna or crabmeat) and a Sandwich Sampler. Serves 10 to 15.

    Appetizer platter

    Wing dings, chicken fingers or buffalo wings and fingers, with mozzarella sticks and fried ravioli. Serves 10.

    Pasta lovers

    Chicken broccoli and ziti, cheese or meat lasagna, chicken, veal or eggplant parmesan, shrimp angelina, shrimp scampi, four-cheese manicotti, stuffed shells, baked ziti, cacciatore and a spaghetti special. Serves 10 to 15.
