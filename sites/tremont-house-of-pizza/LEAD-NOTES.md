# Tremont House of Pizza: redesign concept

Built Oct 29, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated food images.

## Files

- `preview.html`: the whole site in one file, with a "design concept" ribbon. Open it in any browser to show the owner.
- `tremont-house-of-pizza-netlify-site.zip`: the launch-ready site (no ribbon). Unzip and drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## Design concept (v2)

Boat House parallax hero (the shop's own bar photo) plus the **Points Ladder**: tap real menu items, see subtotal, loyalty points earned (1 per $1) and which rewards from the shop's own loyalty page unlock. Nautical navy, rope and brick palette; Arsenal SC + Crimson Pro.

## What we found on the current site

- The menu is a folded-flyer JPEG that can’t be searched, linked or read on a phone. Rebuilt as real text.
- Opening and closing hours are not published anywhere, only “closed Sundays” on the job form.
- The Boat House page has no hours-at-a-glance and no menu link.
- Specials page is still showing 2023 promos.
- Loyalty rewards are a long text page; hard to see what a given order earns.

## What the new site does about it

- Photo parallax hero, readable searchable menu, lunch special with live "on right now" badge, Boat House section, 1978 heritage, awards, community story (The Nick, The Villager), careers, mobile order bar.
- Because no hours are published, the site has no open/closed badge; it says closed Sundays and shows the lunch and bar-seating windows that are on the current site.
- Photos were captured from the shop's own site; confirm the owner is happy to reuse them.
- Search-engine basics: page title and description, Restaurant structured data, sitemap and robots.txt.
