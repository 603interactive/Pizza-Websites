# Mickey's N.Y. Pizza: redesign concept

Built Oct 28, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files

- `preview.html`: the whole site in one file, with a "design concept" ribbon. Open it in any browser to show the owner.
- `mickeys-ny-pizza-netlify-site.zip`: the launch-ready site (no ribbon). Unzip and drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site

- Footer reads "© Crush Pizza", another business's name.
- Every Order Now button opens the same location picker; the Hudson and Derry ordering pages are never linked directly.
- Join Our Team opens a "Coming Soon" page that still contains Wix sample text; there is no way to apply.
- The homepage has no address, hours, phone number or description; the logo and one photo are the only content.
- The menu page is text only, although 70+ of the shop's own food photos already exist in its online ordering menu.

## What the new site does about it

- Order button in the header, the hero, the menu and a sticky phone bar, linking straight into the shop's existing online ordering (with a Hudson / Derry picker).
- Live open / closed status and today's hours for each shop, address, phone and directions.
- Full menu with photos, size prices, category tabs and search, readable on a phone.
- A real job application form (Netlify Forms) replacing the "Coming Soon" page.
- Search-engine basics: page title and description, Restaurant structured data, sitemap and robots.txt.

## Launch steps

1. Get answers to the questions above and update `clients/mickeys-ny-pizza/data.json`, then rebuild with `python3 engine/build.py mickeys-ny-pizza`.
2. Drag the site folder onto Netlify Drop to get a staging link; check it on a phone.
3. Point the shop's domain at Netlify (the owner's domain account is needed).
4. Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and update the Google Business Profile website link.
