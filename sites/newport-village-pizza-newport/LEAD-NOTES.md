# Newport Village Pizza (Newport, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 06, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the lead's content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (newportvillagepizza.com, WordPress)
- Conflicting hours blocks on the same pages (Tue/Wed open vs closed; Sunday open vs closed; 10:30 vs 11:00 starts; one Sunday string says "12 AM").
- Menu HTML duplicates many items; two phone numbers with no explanation; ordering is third-party DoorDash (order.online), not a first-party cart.
- Minor typos in copy and image filenames.

## The idea: Pie Math (receipt-tape planner)
Pick style (cheese pizza/calzone, gourmet pizza/calzone, white pie), size, crust (+$7 gluten-free or cauliflower) and toppings; a receipt tape adds it up using only the shop's published prices. Toppings are "$1-$2 each" on the menu, so the total shows an honest range. Add several pies, add the Game Day Feast, copy the order to text or read it on the phone, or tap to call. Also: Game Day Feast section with an honest comparison (large cheese $14 + mozzarella sticks $8 + bread sticks $6 = $28 menu price before the soda vs. $25 Feast; soda price is not on the menu so it is stated as such), full de-duplicated menu with tabs, search and photos, live open/closed status, hours table, both phone lines labelled Line 1 / Line 2 as the old site prints them, Facebook link, Restaurant structured data, sitemap, robots.txt. Online ordering goes only to the DoorDash order.online URL from the lead.

## Hours (UNVERIFIED, owner must confirm)
Used the homepage banner + matching footer: Sun 11-7, Mon/Thu/Fri 11-8, Sat 12-8, Tue/Wed closed. The brief says the Sunday banner literally reads "12 am - 7 pm" in the HTML and "11 am - 7 pm" in snippets; we used 11. The page tells visitors to call to confirm.

## Menu accuracy
All items and prices transcribed from the shop's menu page as given in the lead. Grinders without their own price use the category sizes (Small $8.75 / Syrian $10.75 / Large or wrap $11.75). The Game Day Feast ($25, Sunday only) comes from the shop's banner image. ZIP 03773 is from public listings, not the shop's site. Email omitted (unverified).
