The shop

On the corner of 16 and 41

White Lake Pizza is open seven days a week at 10 NH Route 41 in West Ossipee. Sit down in the dining room or take it to go, and grab a slice any day from 11 AM to 2 PM.

The menu runs from pizza and calzones to subs, pasta dinners, salads and a kids menu. Try your favorite sub as a wrap or on a bulkie roll.
