    A note from the owner

    Locally owned, not a chain.

    Papa Jay's Pizzeria is a locally owned restaurant that has been open since 2001. We focus on fast service and a quality product, and we have started to offer catering for special events and family gatherings. We offer delivery to Dover, Somersworth, Madbury, and Rollinsford and catering to many other surrounding towns. Unlike chain restaurants we are locally owned, and we use a local produce company, bread company, advertising company and food vendors that employ local people. Without our customers we would not be a successful restaurant. Thank you.

    — Jason Parker, owner · papajays@comcast.net
