# Pelham House of Pizza (Pelham, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 05, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the lead's content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (pelhamhouseofpizza.com, Shift4 SkyTab builder)
- All copy is generic filler; the embedded menu fails ("We couldn't load this store").
- Gallery is Unsplash stock photos, not the shop's food.
- No phone number or hours anywhere; social icons link to generic homepages; email hidden by Cloudflare protection; a bot-check page appears first.

## The idea: the menu board
The shop has black in-store menu screens with red headings. The new menu mirrors them: a dark board with red section bars, tabs and search, so the site menu looks like the counter. Also: live open/closed status, "Today" hours tile, order/call/directions quick bar, sticky mobile call/order dock, hours table, Facebook link (verified in lead), Restaurant structured data, sitemap, robots.txt. Online ordering links to the SkyTab URL from the lead.

## Menu accuracy
The lead had no full menu and the SkyTab page could not be read. Priced items are only the third-party figures in the lead (cheese pizza $11.95 small / $15.95 large, fries $5.95, mozzarella sticks $9.95), labelled as public-listing prices. Other sections list item NAMES read from the blurry in-shop menu board photo (fb-03) with "Call for price": the board prices were too blurry to transcribe reliably, so all were omitted. The lead's steak & cheese sub $12.50 was NOT used because the board appears to show a different price. Some names may be misread; owner should verify the list.
