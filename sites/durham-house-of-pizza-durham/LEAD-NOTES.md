# Durham House of Pizza (DHOP), Durham NH: redesign concept

Built Nov 04, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files

- `preview.html`: the whole site in one file, with a "design concept" ribbon. Open in any browser to show the owner.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (durhamhouseofpizza.com)

- Unedited Wix restaurant template ("© 2023 by The Pizza Shop"), with placeholder headings and one short paragraph; nothing about 1976, UNH or the late hours.
- "Join Our Team" page is empty (no text, no form).
- A separate "Unofficial Website" (dhoppizza.com) lists 42 Main Street and different hours (open Sundays), competing in search.
- Menu typos: Chicken Cacciatore shows a 10" Super (we used 20"), "Cheesey Breatsticks"; size labels inconsistent.
- Hours text broken ("9 :00P M"); no map, no storefront photo up top, no social links.

## The one big idea: The Late Shift

Thursday to Saturday the shop is open until 2 AM, which almost nobody else in town is, and the current site never says so. The new site leads with it: a week-at-a-glance chart plus a slider you drag to any time of day to see which nights the kitchen is open, with a live "Now" marker. After-midnight hours are shown on the night they start (Thu hours run to Fri 2 AM). The live open/closed pill handles the 2 AM close correctly and was tested at 12:30 AM, 1 AM, 2:30 AM and on closed Sundays.
