# Alton Village Pizza (Alton, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 06, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the lead's content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (altonvillagepizza.com, Joomla + PCS Web Design template)
- Dated template; menu shown mostly as large graphic boards that are hard to read on phones (prices also exist in HTML).
- Call button shows "875.7333" without area code; street address not in plain text (PO Box and a map only); email cloaked; no delivery info.

## The idea: Pizza Price Check on a gingham tablecloth
Look: red/white gingham like the shop's own tablecloths in its photos, Marconi olive-oil green, paper-placemat menu with tabs and search, polaroid-style photo cards.
Selling point: a "Pizza Price Check" tool. Pick small/large, toppings (the shop's 22 listed), extra cheese/sauce, gluten free, and it totals from the menu's real prices. It then tells you honestly when a specialty (Vegetarian, or Village Special at 8+ toppings) costs less than building the same pizza, and a table shows the math (Village Special saves $6.50 small / $11.00 large vs. 8 built toppings; Vegetarian saves $3.76 / $7.00 vs. 6). "Copy my pizza" gives text to read when calling or paste into the online order. Also: live open/closed status, today's hours, sticky mobile call/order dock, full menu (all sections from the lead), Restaurant structured data, sitemap, robots.txt. Ordering links only to Big Holler (from the lead).

## Menu accuracy
All prices come from the lead's transcription of the shop's menu page HTML; labeled "from the shop's website menu, may have changed". Menu-board graphics (400px) were not used. Vegetarian-set matching assumes "Peppers" = Pepper and "Mushrooms" = Fresh Mushrooms. The Village Special comparison assumes any 8 toppings; the shop's actual mix is not listed. Gluten free +$4.50 is added to both builds and specialties in the tool; confirm it applies to specialties. French fries show small/large; two single-price gyro subs are shown as one price. Beer/wine listed as on the menu (21+).
