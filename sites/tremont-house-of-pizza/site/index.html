      The Boat House

      A 13-seat bar, 12 taps.

      • In 2024 the shop took over the entire building: more dine-in seating plus a 13-seat, full-service bar.
      • Named the Boat House for the bar’s unique shape and decor.
      • A 12-tap reverse draft system, plus wine, cans and bottled beer.
      • A cocktail of the month for the non-wine, non-beer crowd.
      Monday – FridayBar seating opens at 3 PM and stays open to close.
      SaturdayBar seating all day, 11 AM to close.
      Any open hourEvery drink on the menu is available whenever the shop is open.

      The Nick

      A pesto, chicken, tomato and spinach pizza made at Tremont. In 2016, 20% of September sales went to CHAD for Team Nick. It was such a hit that it joined the menu. The pesto comes from VegetaBall’s in Claremont.

      The Villager

      A one-month grinder special: 20% of sales were donated as gas cards for Norris Cotton Cancer Center patients. 127 were sold in October and $190.49 went to the NCCC.
