# Seabrook House of Pizza (Seabrook, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 05, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (seabrookpizza.com, GoDaddy Website Builder)
- One-screen page: the tagline repeated 8 times in a slideshow, Order Now / Call Us / Directions buttons, email signup. Nothing else.
- No menu, no hours, no street address, no about text, no food photos. Phone number only appears on a button.
- Sloppy copy ("CALZONES, salads, subs seafood, Roast Beef ,gyro", "DINE IN .TAKEOUT."); no Facebook/Instagram links.
- Old domain seabrookhop.com redirects to a parked page but is still indexed as "Seabrook House of Pizza - Specials".

## The idea
A bright, coastal-diner look (Coca-Cola red, harbor navy, sand, taken from the storefront signs and umbrellas). The hero leads with a real pizza photo. A "Three ways to eat" band (dine in / takeout / delivery, the shop's own words) puts the three real actions up front: Toast order link, Uber Eats link, directions. "What are you craving?" is a tabbed, searchable menu browser built on the 31 category names from the Uber Eats listing, each with a real photo and an order link. Live open/closed pill, sticky call/order/directions bar on phones, Facebook/Instagram/Uber Eats links (all verified in the lead), Restaurant structured data, sitemap, robots.txt.

## IMPORTANT: menu has NO item names or prices
The lead has only category names (Toast blocks automated reading; no menu images were provided). Nothing was invented: tabs show categories and send people to Toast for items and prices. Needs the real menu from the owner/Toast before this is a complete site.

## Fixes / judgment calls
- Hours: only "10 AM - 9 PM" from Uber Eats, days unknown. The site shows 10-9 for all 7 days, labeled "as listed on Uber Eats, please call to confirm". The live open/closed pill uses this assumption.
- Address "Unit 9" comes from Uber Eats; the shop's site shows none.
- Tagline wording cleaned up (capitalization/spacing) only.
- The partial Instagram special ("Cheeseburger, French Fries & 20oz ... 8.85") was NOT used because the text is cut off.
