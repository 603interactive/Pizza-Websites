# Upper Crust Pizzeria (Tilton, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 04, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (uppercrustpizzanh.com, Wix)
- Unedited Wix restaurant template: footer "(c) 2023 by The Pizza Shop. Proudly created with Wix.com".
- Menu page still shows "I'm a paragraph. Click here to add your own text..." placeholder; a blank "New Page" and a second "My New Menu" tab are in the nav.
- "Our Story" / "Passion for Pizza" headings have no story text; Reservations and Private Events sections have no details.
- Every image is Wix stock photography; none show the shop's food or building.
- Hours block is misaligned and the split Wed-Thu shifts are hard to read; no address in the header, no map or directions; typo "Tomatos".
- Only calls to action: mailing-list signup and the Slice link.

## The one big idea: the Week Board
The shop runs split shifts (Tue 4-8, Wed-Thu 12-2 and 4-8, Fri-Sat 12-9, Sun 12-8, Mon closed), which is exactly what the current site shows badly. The new site draws the week as a chalkboard timeline with the afternoon break visible, a "When are you hungry?" slider that shows which days are open at any time, and a live open/closed status that handles the break correctly.

## Also on the site
Full menu (12 sections, real prices, search), mobile nav and sticky call/order bar, address, phone, directions link, Instagram link, Restaurant structured data, sitemap and robots.txt. Ordering links to the Slice URL from the lead.
