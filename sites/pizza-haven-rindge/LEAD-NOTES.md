# Pizza Haven (Rindge, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 04, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files

- `preview.html`: the whole site in one file, with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: the launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (pizzahavenrindge.com)

- Template copied from a Massachusetts pizzeria: homepage says "The best place to eat in Stoughton, MA!" twice and the contact page names "Runner Pizza Stoughton".
- A Facebook icon links to Runner Pizza Stoughton; other social icons go to generic homepages.
- Unedited "Porto" theme demo content and stock demo photos; filler copy, typo "costumers".
- Address typo "51343 NH-119", hours typo "11:000am", weekend hours missing.
- Dead "Latest Tweets: Please wait..." widget and a "Success! You've been added to our email list" message visible by default.
- Only two pages (Home, Contact); no menu on the site itself, everything is pushed to orderpizzahaven.com.
- Ordering menu data errors: "Mac & Cheese Pizza" described as a burger pizza and "Macaroni & Cheese Pizza" described as spaghetti/ziti.

## The one big idea

Pick a Pizza With Attitude: the shop lists 34 pizzas (cheese to cheeseburger, taco, shrimp, mac and cheese) but its menu is one long list. Visitors filter by craving (classic cheese, chicken, steak and beef, pepperoni and bacon, no meat, wild cards) or hit "Surprise me" for a random pick with its real price and the matching calzone price. Backed by a "Two pies. One price." section built from the two real coupons ($24.99 and $26.99), and the full menu with prices, tabs and search. Filter tags are our reading of each pizza's listed toppings.

## What else the new site does

- Live open/closed status for the only published hours (Mon-Fri 11 AM-9 PM); weekends say "not confirmed, call" instead of guessing.
- Address corrected to 1343 NH-119, phone, directions (maps search link, no verified coordinates), sticky call/order bar on phones.
- Order links all go to the shop's own https://orderpizzahaven.com/.
- Accessible: alt text, focus styles, landmarks, skip link; Restaurant structured data, sitemap, robots.txt.
