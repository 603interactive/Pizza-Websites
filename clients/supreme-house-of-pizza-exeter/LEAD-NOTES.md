# Supreme House of Pizza / Supreme Pizza & Seafood (Exeter, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 06, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the lead's content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (supremepizzaexeter.net, stock FoodTec "Latte" theme)
- It works as an ordering shell, not a branded site: pickup/delivery timers, tracker and subscribe box dominate the home page.
- About copy is generic template voice; carousel uses decorative backgrounds; no Facebook/Instagram links in the header; stale reference to supreme-pizza.com.
- Sunday hours meta tags say 9pm but the visible table and public listings say 8pm. We used 8pm.

## The idea: one shop, two halves, and a tray
The shop's logo is a circle split between a red pizza slice and a blue fish, so the site is split red (pizza) and sea blue (seafood), with the real logo at the seam. Selling-point feature: **the order tray**. Every price on the full menu (236 items, real prices from the lead) is tappable and builds a running estimate. The tray also checks the shop's own free-pizza specials (buy 2 large: free small cheese; buy 4 large: free large cheese), shows rewards points per the shop's posted terms ($1 = 1 point, 100 points = $5 off, double Tuesdays), and offers "Copy my list" to read over the phone or text. It sends nothing to the shop; "Order this online" goes to the shop's FoodTec link. Also: live open/closed status, sticky mobile call/order/directions dock, search, Restaurant structured data.

## Menu accuracy
All items and prices come from the FoodTec menu text in the lead. Cleaned obvious typos (Romane, Getorade, Saucee, Cordon Blue, Limoncello, Aquafine -> Aquafina, Galaktobouriko -> Galaktoboureko), merged duplicated blocks (duplicate Jalapeno Poppers, the Steak Subs list that appeared twice), and condensed drinks. Free-pizza specials have no price and are labeled "Applies when you order". Pickup 15 min / delivery 45 min, 10% off first online order, and no-contact delivery are quoted from the shop's site and labeled as such. How the free-pizza specials are applied at checkout is not known; the tray says to check at checkout.

## IMAGE RIGHTS FLAG

Facebook page link is listed in the lead as public.

5. Hours: Mon-Sat 11-9, Sun 11-8 correct? Holiday hours?
6. Is the rewards program ($1 = 1 point, 100 points = $5, double Tuesdays) and the 10% first-order offer still live?

8. Okay to point "Order online" at the FoodTec link, and may we add Facebook?
