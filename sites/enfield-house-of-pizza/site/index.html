A family affair

Greek family recipes, an Enfield community shop.

Great food, friends, family, community. “This is our community,” says owner Lori. “We love being a part of it.”

  1. 1987

    Alex Korakas opens Enfield House of Pizza. Lori Bliss Hill becomes his first employee while still a student at Mascoma Valley Regional High School.

  2. 1991

    Lori becomes sole owner. Alex passes on his long-held secret family recipes from Greece for the special pizza dough and sauce, still made today.

  3. 2001

    Lori buys property half a mile east of the original shop. The new Enfield House of Pizza opens July 4, 2001.

  4. Today

    A community place: Mascoma High Project Graduation, Enfield Library Summer Reading, the Enfield Family Fair, David’s House Annual Auction, Toys for Tots and town celebrations. Lifetime members of the Royals Athletic Boosters. #fuelyourcommunity
