# Mary's Pizza (Gorham, NH): redesign concept

Built Nov 05, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only (lead brief, July 2026 takeout menu images, shop website and Facebook photos). No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files
- `preview.html`: whole site in one file with a "design concept" ribbon.
- `site/`: launch-ready site (no ribbon). Drag onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site (maryspizzanh.com, GoDaddy Website Builder)
- Footer still reads "Copyright (c) 2018".
- The online-ordering link in the footer is a raw, mangled Proofpoint "urldefense" email-scanner URL printed as text, clearly pasted from an email.
- The menu exists only as downloadable PDFs made in Illustrator: not searchable and hard to read on a phone. The "Menu" nav item just opens the download page.
- Homepage is thin (name repeated as headings, one welcome line, an 8-slide carousel, the awards). The mobile header repeats "Mary's Pizza" several times.
- No Facebook link, no delivery or catering info. The 79-year family story is buried on a plain About page.

## The idea
A gingham-tablecloth, family-recipe-card look (the shop's red-and-white checked tablecloths appear in its own photos), with the chef logo from the menu and the awards up front. Sections: live open/closed status that handles the lunch and dinner split shifts, a "what's the deal today" card driven by the day of the week (Everyday $10 lunch, Tuesday and Thursday specials), the full menu as 11 tabs with search and every price, a **Build a pizza, see the price** calculator that uses the shop's own Create Your Own price list (size, crust, up to any number of toppings, extra cheese or sauce), the family story with the four-generation line, hours, address, tel: link, directions link, order button and Facebook link. Restaurant structured data, sitemap and robots.txt included.
