# Golden Acres Pizza Market: redesign concept

Built Oct 28, 2026 by 603 Interactive from the shop's own content only. No AI-generated or stock food photos.

## Files

- `preview.html`: the whole site in one file, with a "design concept" ribbon. Open it in any browser to show the owner.
- `golden-acres-pizza-market-netlify-site.zip`: the launch-ready site (no ribbon). Unzip and drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.

## What we found on the current site

- The Menu page shows raw page-builder code ("[ux_banner height=…]", "[button text=\"Order now\"…]") instead of a menu; the About page does the same.
- Navigation has WordPress leftovers: a "#75 (no title)" link, "Style" and "Uncategorized" category links, and a "Hello world!" sample post.
- The phone number shows unformatted as 6036230899, twice, in a strip of tiny header links.
- The homepage still says "family-owned for 24 years"; the shop opened in 1998.
- Footer reads "Fast Food WordPress Theme By Buywptemplate".
- The menu page's Order Now button points at a Toast page while the header points at FoodTec; FoodTec is the live one.
- The site has 50+ real photos of the food, the owner and the shop sitting unused in its media library.

## What the new site does about it

- Order button in the header, the hero, the menu and a sticky phone bar, linking straight into the shop's existing online ordering.
- Live open / closed status and today's hours for each shop, address, phone and directions.
- Full menu with photos, size prices, category tabs and search, readable on a phone.
- Search-engine basics: page title and description, Restaurant structured data, sitemap and robots.txt.

## Launch steps

1. Get answers to the questions above and update `clients/golden-acres-pizza-market/data.json`, then rebuild with `python3 engine/build.py golden-acres-pizza-market`.
2. Drag the site folder onto Netlify Drop to get a staging link; check it on a phone.
3. Point the shop's domain at Netlify (the owner's domain account is needed).
4. Submit `sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and update the Google Business Profile website link.

## Design concept (v2)
- Look: bright whitewash and Aegean blue with a Greek-key border, menu laid out as market aisles, Gloock + Instrument Sans.
- Selling point: the **Size Lab**. Pick any pizza; the 14" and 20" are drawn to true proportion with area in square inches and cents per square inch from the shop's own prices (20" is about 2.0x the pizza for 1.3-1.4x the price), plus a best-value ranking of every pie.
- Real photos are used for Meat Lovers, Margherita and Buffalo & BBQ; other pies are drawn in code from the menu description (no stock or AI images).
- Open question: confirm the 14"/20" prices are current (math updates by re-running the build).
